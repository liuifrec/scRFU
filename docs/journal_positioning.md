# Journal positioning

## 1. Cell Reports Methods

Best fit if the final work demonstrates a transferable method with exact
technical fidelity, scalable execution, a compelling repeated-measures use case,
independent public validation, held-out transfer, and complete reproducibility.
The narrative should emphasize receptor-state representation and validated
workflows, not software convenience alone.

## 2. PLOS Computational Biology

Strong fit if the methodological definitions, null/robustness analyses,
comparators, and biological interpretation are sufficiently general. Public
data, transparent source tables, and careful claims about donor-level inference
are essential.

## 3. NAR Genomics and Bioinformatics

Appropriate if the principal strength is a robust open genomics software and
benchmark framework with broad input compatibility, transfer diagnostics, and
reproducible applications.

## Claim discipline

scRFU does not replace or invent RFU. Original RFU supplies the representation
and reference assignment method; scRFU supplies standardized inputs,
deterministic orchestration, provenance, sample/cell reconstruction,
longitudinal analysis, transfer diagnostics, phenotype/antigen evidence, and
benchmarking. Threshold failure is low reference similarity, not validated OOD.
VDJdb matches are evidence, not specificity proof. BCR is omitted unless its
separate reference and held-out validation gates pass.
